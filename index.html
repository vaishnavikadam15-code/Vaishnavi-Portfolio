Data Analytics Case Study

Hotel Booking Analytics Dashboard

119K+ Rows Analyzed

01 / The Problem

Hospitality operations struggle with unorganized booking patterns, high cancellation rates, and hidden revenue leakage. Management needed actionable clarity from over 119,000 reservation records to optimize pricing and guest retention strategies.

02 / What I Built

An end-to-end interactive Power BI dashboard featuring dynamic DAX measures, trend forecasting, customer segmentation models, and cancellation risk indicators.

03 / How It Works

Raw CSV reservation data was cleaned and modeled in Excel/Power Query, relationships established between guest demographics and lead times, and DAX formulas calculated cancellation ratios and revenue metrics dynamically upon filter selection.
